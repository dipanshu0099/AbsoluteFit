Rest Intervals

  • Compound lifts — 90–120 sec
  • Isolation exercises — 60–90 sec
  • Fat Loss circuits — 30–45 sec
  • Weight Gain (5×5) — 2–3 min
  • Core exercises — 30–60 sec

General Rules

  • Warm up 5–10 min before every session
  • Cool down with 5 min static stretching
  • Drink 3–4 litres of water daily
  • Form > Weight — always
  • Sleep 7–9 hours for recovery

Weekly Schedule

  • Train 5 days / week
  • Day 6 & 7 — Active recovery
  • Light walk, stretching, yoga on rest days
  • Same time each day builds consistency

Progressive Overload

  • Increase weight by 2.5–5 kg when reps feel easy
  • Log your weights every session
  • Consistency beats perfection
  • Results show in 4–8 weeks

Supplements

  • Whey protein — post-workout
  • Creatine — 3–5g/day (monohydrate)
  • Caffeine — 100–200mg pre-workout
  • Omega-3 & Vitamin D3 — daily
  • Food first. Supplements are secondary.
